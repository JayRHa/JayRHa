### 👋 About Me

I'm **Jannik Reinhard** — a **dual Microsoft MVP** (Security · Intune and AI Platform · Azure AI Services) and **Head of AI at Epic Fusion**. I build practical tooling at the intersection of **modern device management**, **automation**, and **AI-driven IT operations** — including an AI framework serving **70k+ active users**.

- 🔭 Currently working on **agentic workflows & AI-powered endpoint operations**
- 🌐 I write about Intune, Azure AI & automation on my blog → **[jannikreinhard.com](https://jannikreinhard.com/)**
- 🎤 Speaker at **MMS, Workplace Ninja, Modern Endpoint Management Summit** & more
- 💬 Active in the largest **Intune communities** on LinkedIn & Discord
- ⚡ Fun fact: I automate everything I touch — even my coffee machine has a CLI
